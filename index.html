A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Why I make games

I'm studying game development at YRGO in Gothenburg, Sweden. Growing up with games like Stardew Valley, Terraria and Elden Ring made me especially passionate about RPGs and exploration.

I want to create worlds where exploration stays interesting, even towards the end of an adventure. I enjoy designing systems, combining shaders, animation and particles, and experimenting with new technologies.
